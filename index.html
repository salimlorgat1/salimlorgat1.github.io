Synopsis

Drawing upon lived experience, community memory, and social observation, Surviving the Burden offers a compelling account of identity, family, faith, duty, and belonging within the Gujarati Muslim diaspora in Britain. It is a valuable record of memory, resilience, and truth, and an important contribution to the understanding of the migrant experience and the communities that helped shape modern Britain.

About the Author

Salim Yusuf Lorgat was born in Gujarat, India, and later settled in Blackburn, Lancashire. Throughout his adult life, he has been active in community service, local government, charitable work, and public affairs. A former councillor, charity trustee, and community volunteer, he has spent many years reflecting on migration, identity, family expectations, and the experiences of the Gujarati Muslim diaspora in Britain. Surviving the Burden is his first book.
